The Problem

Why Traditional Feeders
Fall Short

Species Dominance

Aggressive bird species bully timid ones away from conventional feeders, creating an unbalanced ecosystem at your backyard station.

Seed Waste

First-come-first-serve buffet-style feeders lead to massive seed spillage and waste, with dominant visitors gorging indiscriminately.

Disease Spread

Leftover seed accumulation becomes a breeding ground for avian diseases, putting entire local bird populations at risk.

Squirrel Raids

Squirrels consume vast quantities of bird feed, driving up costs and preventing target species from accessing nutrition.

“Conventional seed trays are a first-come-first-serve buffet. Dominant birds elbow out the timid, wasted seed piles up, and diseases thrive in leftovers.”

OrniMetrics solves all of this with AI-powered selective feeding.

Process

How OrniMetrics
Works

A seamless, automated pipeline from detection to dispensing

01

Boot & Initialize

System powers on, loads the YOLO model onto the Coral TPU, and initializes the servo mechanism and camera feed.

02

Continuous Scanning

The camera continuously monitors the feeder area, running real-time inference to detect any animal presence.

03

AI Classification

The custom YOLO model classifies visitors as specific bird species or squirrels, with per-species confidence thresholds.

04

Smart Dispensing

Species-specific servo commands release the right food portion. Squirrels trigger a different response. Each event is logged and photographed.

05

Cooldown & Repeat

Per-species cooldown timers prevent over-feeding and resource exhaustion, then the system returns to scanning.

Hardware

Parts
Tour

Smart, safe, and serviceable — every component custom-engineered

01

Tray / Baseplate

Custom-designed baseplate with textured grip surfaces for comfortable bird perching. Secured with five 3mm mounting screws to lock the dispensing mechanism in place. Engineered to support the combined weight of stored feed and landing birds.

5x 3mm screws Grip-optimized
02

Dispensing Mechanism

Precision servo-driven dispensing system controlled by the PCA9685 HAT. Features jitter-safe timing with configurable slew/ramp rates for smooth food release. Per-species portion control through software-defined servo angles.

Slew-rate control Jitter-safe
03

Feed Storage

Weatherproof storage compartment designed to keep bird seed dry and fresh. Gravity-fed delivery system ensures consistent food supply to the dispensing mechanism. Capacity optimized for multi-day autonomous operation.

Weatherproof Gravity-fed
04

Camera Housing

Protective enclosure for the detection camera, providing optimal viewing angle while shielding electronics from weather. Houses the camera module that feeds the YOLO detection pipeline at up to 12 FPS.

Up to 12 FPS Weather-sealed
